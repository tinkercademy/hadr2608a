Presentations and conclusion

Prepare to present your work to the class with your group.

Build the slide deck

Use Claude Design to build a short slide deck. Give it your PRD.md, README.md, screenshots, and test evidence.

Use this prompt:

Create a short slide deck for our LLM app. Explain the problem, show the input-to-output path, identify the three course techniques, and show one item of evidence. Use clear visuals and little text. Do not invent features, results, or measurements.

Review all generated text and images. Correct errors before you present.

Resources:

  • Get started with Claude Design
  • Use Claude Design for presentations and slide decks

Quiz gate

Before you present, each team member must show that they understand the app. Open the project in Claude Code and use this prompt:

Give me an HTML report on this app with context and intuition, and a five-question quiz at the bottom. I will not present until I score five out of five.

Each team member must score five out of five. If you do not pass, review the app and take the quiz again.

Present

Make the result easy to understand. Use the slide deck and show the working app if possible. Explain the three course techniques and one thing that the team learned.
